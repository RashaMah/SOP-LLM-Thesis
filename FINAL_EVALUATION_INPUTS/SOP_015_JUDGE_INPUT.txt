EVALUATION PRINCIPLES
============================================================

   never provided in the source scenario.

5. Evaluate only the candidate SOP provided.

6. Do not infer, identify, or speculate about which language model

7. Score each criterion independently.

8. Base each score on concrete comparison between the SOURCE SCENARIO and
   the CANDIDATE SOP.

9. Do not reward verbosity, document length, sophisticated formatting,

11. Do not compare the candidate SOP with other candidate SOPs.

12. Do not calculate or provide an overall score or model ranking.

C1 — COMPLETENESS
============================================================

Evaluate whether all operationally relevant information explicitly
contained in the source scenario is represented in the SOP, including
required activities, information, roles, conditions, and expected outcomes.

Do not penalize information that was not provided in the source scenario.


============================================================
C2 — CORRECT SEQUENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether procedural activities follow the operational dependencies
and sequence specified or implied by the source scenario.

Penalize contradictory, illogical, or operationally incorrect ordering.


============================================================
C3 — ROLE CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether responsibilities are clearly and consistently assigned
to roles provided in the source scenario.

Penalize ambiguous responsibility, incorrect role assignment, or
unsupported new roles when these materially affect the procedure.


============================================================
C4 — INPUT / OUTPUT CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether required inputs and information, as well as expected
outputs and results, are clearly identifiable and consistent with the
source scenario.


============================================================
C5 — EXCEPTION HANDLING
============================================================

Evaluate whether exceptions, deviations, abnormal conditions, and
decision points contained in the source scenario are correctly identified
and handled.

Do not penalize the SOP merely because the source scenario itself contains
few exceptions.


============================================================
C6 — CONSTRAINT ADHERENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether explicit constraints and decision conditions contained
in the source scenario are preserved and respected throughout the SOP.


============================================================
C7 — GROUNDEDNESS / NO HALLUCINATION
============================================================

Evaluate whether operational claims in the SOP remain grounded in the
source scenario.

Penalize invented systems, roles, requirements, thresholds, documents,
technologies, approvals, rules, or other material operational details
that are unsupported by the source scenario.

Do not penalize harmless paraphrasing, organizational formatting, or
wording changes that preserve the original operational meaning.


============================================================
C8 — USABILITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether the SOP is clear, structured, internally consistent,
unambiguous, and actionable for the intended warehouse or logistics user,
while remaining faithful to the source scenario.


============================================================
SCORING SCALE
============================================================

5 — FULLY SATISFIES
The SOP fully satisfies the criterion. No substantive deficiency is
identifiable.

4 — GOOD
The SOP satisfies the criterion with only a minor omission, ambiguity,
or weakness that does not materially affect the procedure.

3 — ADEQUATE
The SOP partially satisfies the criterion. One meaningful deficiency or
several minor deficiencies are present, but the SOP remains substantially
correct and usable on this dimension.

2 — POOR
Major deficiencies are present. Important information, logic, or
requirements are missing, unclear, or incorrect and materially affect
this dimension.

1 — VERY POOR
The SOP fundamentally fails the criterion, substantially contradicts the
source scenario, or omits or incorrectly handles most essential elements
relevant to the criterion.

Process: Order Picking

The picking instruction requests an item that is not physically available at its expected picking location.

CANDIDATE SOP:

# Standard Operating Procedure: Order Picking – Handling Unavailable Stock

## 1. SOP Title
Order Picking Procedure: Handling Unavailable Stock at Expected Location

## 2. Purpose
To ensure the correct continuation of multi-item picking operations when a requested item is not physically available at its expected picking location. This procedure ensures that unavailable stock is not substituted or invented, discrepancies are properly recorded and communicated, and available items are processed correctly.

## 3. Scope
This procedure applies to all multi-item picking operations within the warehouse where a picker encounters a situation where the required quantity of an item is not physically available at the listed location. It involves the Picker/Storekeeper, the Storekeeper responsible for stock records, and the Warehouse Supervisor/Manager.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Picker/Storekeeper:**
    *   Follows the authorized picking instruction.
    *   Verifies physical availability of items at listed locations.
    *   Records discrepancies between physical location and inventory/picking information.
    *   Communicates discrepancies to responsible warehouse personnel.
    *   Picks available items correctly.
    *   Ensures missing quantities are not falsely marked as picked.
*   **Storekeeper responsible for stock records:**
    *   Receives communication regarding stock discrepancies.
    *   Checks and reconciles stock records upon notification.
*   **Warehouse Supervisor/Manager:**
    *   Acts as responsible warehouse personnel for escalation.
    *   Oversees stock reconciliation or back-order handling processes.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
*   Authorized picking instruction containing item locations and quantities.
*   Physical inventory present at the picking location.
*   Warehouse stock rules regarding batch or quantity selection.

## 6. Expected Outputs
*   Available items picked correctly according to the instruction.
*   Unavailable quantities not falsely confirmed as picked.
*   Recorded discrepancy between physical location and inventory/picking information.
*   Escalation of the discrepancy for stock reconciliation or back-order handling.

## 7. Procedure
1.  **Receive Instruction:** Obtain the authorized picking instruction for the order.
2.  **Navigate to Location:** Proceed to the listed location for each item specified in the picking instruction.
3.  **Verify Availability:** Physically verify the presence and quantity of the requested item at the location.
4.  **Pick Available Items:** If the required quantity is physically available, pick the item and proceed to the next item in the instruction.
5.  **Identify Unavailability:** If the required quantity is not physically available at the expected location, stop the picking action for that specific item.
6.  **Adhere to Substitution Rules:** Do not select an alternative batch or quantity unless explicitly permitted by the warehouse stock rules.
7.  **Record Discrepancy:** Document the discrepancy between the physical location and the inventory/picking information.
8.  **Communicate Discrepancy:** Notify the responsible warehouse personnel (Storekeeper responsible for stock records or Warehouse Supervisor/Manager) regarding the unavailability.
9.  **Continue Operation:** Resume the picking operation for other items in the picking instruction that are available.
10. **Prevent False Confirmation:** Ensure the system or record does not indicate that the missing quantity has been picked.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
*   **Unavailable Stock:** When an item is not found at the expected location, the picker must not attempt to fulfill the requirement using stock from an unauthorized location or by estimating quantity.
*   **Substitution Prohibition:** An alternative batch or quantity must not be selected unless permitted by the warehouse stock rules. If rules do not permit substitution, the item remains unpicked.
*   **Recording Requirement:** The discrepancy must be recorded immediately upon discovery. Failure to record prevents stock records from being checked and reconciled.
*   **Escalation:** All instances of unavailable stock must be communicated to the Storekeeper responsible for stock records or Warehouse Supervisor/Manager to facilitate stock reconciliation or back-order handling.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The picking process is considered complete when:
1.  All available items identified in the picking instruction have been picked correctly.
2.  Unavailable quantities have not been falsely confirmed as picked.
3.  The discrepancy regarding unavailable stock has been recorded and escalated to the responsible warehouse personnel for stock reconciliation or back-order handling.
