You are an evaluator of generated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
for logistics and warehouse processes.

Your task is to evaluate ONE candidate SOP strictly against the supplied
SOURCE SCENARIO using the supplied eight-criterion evaluation rubric.

============================================================
EVALUATION PRINCIPLES
============================================================

1. Treat the SOURCE SCENARIO as the sole factual reference.

2. Do not use external operational knowledge to impose requirements that
   are absent from the source scenario.

3. Do not reward the candidate SOP for adding plausible but unsupported
   operational details.

4. Do not penalize the candidate SOP for omitting information that was
   never provided in the source scenario.

5. Evaluate only the candidate SOP provided.

6. Do not infer, identify, or speculate about which language model
   generated the candidate SOP.

7. Score each criterion independently.

8. Base each score on concrete comparison between the SOURCE SCENARIO and
   the CANDIDATE SOP.

9. Do not reward verbosity, document length, sophisticated formatting,
   or writing style unless these materially improve the criterion being
   evaluated.

10. Reasonable paraphrasing is acceptable when the operational meaning
    of the source scenario is preserved.

11. Do not compare the candidate SOP with other candidate SOPs.

12. Do not calculate or provide an overall score or model ranking.


============================================================
C1 — COMPLETENESS
============================================================

Evaluate whether all operationally relevant information explicitly
contained in the source scenario is represented in the SOP, including
required activities, information, roles, conditions, and expected outcomes.

Do not penalize information that was not provided in the source scenario.


============================================================
C2 — CORRECT SEQUENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether procedural activities follow the operational dependencies
and sequence specified or implied by the source scenario.

Penalize contradictory, illogical, or operationally incorrect ordering.


============================================================
C3 — ROLE CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether responsibilities are clearly and consistently assigned
to roles provided in the source scenario.

Penalize ambiguous responsibility, incorrect role assignment, or
unsupported new roles when these materially affect the procedure.


============================================================
C4 — INPUT / OUTPUT CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether required inputs and information, as well as expected
outputs and results, are clearly identifiable and consistent with the
source scenario.


============================================================
C5 — EXCEPTION HANDLING
============================================================

Evaluate whether exceptions, deviations, abnormal conditions, and
decision points contained in the source scenario are correctly identified
and handled.

Do not penalize the SOP merely because the source scenario itself contains
few exceptions.


============================================================
C6 — CONSTRAINT ADHERENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether explicit constraints and decision conditions contained
in the source scenario are preserved and respected throughout the SOP.


============================================================
C7 — GROUNDEDNESS / NO HALLUCINATION
============================================================

Evaluate whether operational claims in the SOP remain grounded in the
source scenario.

Penalize invented systems, roles, requirements, thresholds, documents,
technologies, approvals, rules, or other material operational details
that are unsupported by the source scenario.

Do not penalize harmless paraphrasing, organizational formatting, or
wording changes that preserve the original operational meaning.


============================================================
C8 — USABILITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether the SOP is clear, structured, internally consistent,
unambiguous, and actionable for the intended warehouse or logistics user,
while remaining faithful to the source scenario.


============================================================
SCORING SCALE
============================================================

5 — FULLY SATISFIES
The SOP fully satisfies the criterion. No substantive deficiency is
identifiable.

4 — GOOD
The SOP satisfies the criterion with only a minor omission, ambiguity,
or weakness that does not materially affect the procedure.

3 — ADEQUATE
The SOP partially satisfies the criterion. One meaningful deficiency or
several minor deficiencies are present, but the SOP remains substantially
correct and usable on this dimension.

2 — POOR
Major deficiencies are present. Important information, logic, or
requirements are missing, unclear, or incorrect and materially affect
this dimension.

1 — VERY POOR
The SOP fundamentally fails the criterion, substantially contradicts the
source scenario, or omits or incorrectly handles most essential elements
relevant to the criterion.


============================================================
SCORING PROCEDURE
============================================================

For EACH of the eight criteria:

1. Compare the candidate SOP directly with the source scenario.
2. Identify relevant evidence.
3. Identify substantive omissions, contradictions, unsupported additions,
   or ambiguities relevant to that criterion.
4. Assign exactly one INTEGER score from 1 to 5.
5. Provide a concise justification explaining the score.

Evaluate all eight criteria even if deficiencies overlap.

Do not calculate the total score. The experimental evaluation script will
calculate totals after the individual criterion scores have been recorded.

# Standard Operating Procedure: Packing and Dispatch Preparation

## 1. SOP Title

**Packing and Dispatch Preparation**

## 2. Purpose

To ensure that correctly picked orders are packed, documented, staged, verified, and released accurately for transport, with corresponding stock records updated.

## 3. Scope

This SOP applies to completed picking orders moved to the staging area for packing and shipment. It covers:

- Separation and identification of staged orders
- Packing of picked items
- Association of packed goods with the authorized order
- Preparation of the packing list and required transport/release documentation
- Verification of the shipment against dispatch documentation
- Verification of outgoing quantities
- Transfer of custody to the carrier or recipient
- Updating of stock records for released goods

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### 4.1 Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper

- Maintain clear separation of orders in the staging area.
- Confirm that goods being packed are associated with the correct authorized order.
- Pack the items appropriately.
- Prepare the packing list and required transport/release documentation.
- Make the shipment available for checking and release.
- Ensure stock records reflect the goods released.

### 4.2 Warehouse or Store Manager

- Oversee the packing and dispatch preparation process.
- Confirm that the shipment is ready for release after required checks are completed.
- Address identified discrepancies or deviations before release.

### 4.3 Loading/Checking Personnel

- Check the prepared shipment against the dispatch documentation.
- Verify the outgoing quantity before custody is transferred.
- Load or prepare the verified shipment for handover.

### 4.4 Transport Representative

- Receive custody of the verified shipment for transport.
- Participate in the handover after the outgoing quantity has been verified.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information

- A correctly picked and completed order
- The authorized order information
- Goods moved to the staging area
- Information required to prepare the packing list
- Information required for transport/release documentation
- Dispatch documentation used to verify the shipment
- Outgoing quantity information
- Stock records requiring adjustment for released goods

## 6. Expected Outputs

- Correct goods packed appropriately
- Packed goods clearly associated with the authorized order
- Orders kept clearly separated in the staging area
- Completed packing list
- Completed required transport/release documentation
- Shipment verified against dispatch documentation
- Outgoing quantity verified before transfer of custody
- Shipment handed over for transport
- Stock records accurately reflecting the released goods

## 7. Procedure

### 7.1 Receive the Completed Order in the Staging Area

1. Confirm that the completed picking order has been moved to the staging area.
2. Keep the order clearly separated from all other orders in the staging area.
3. Maintain clear association between the goods and the corresponding authorized order throughout packing and dispatch preparation.

### 7.2 Confirm the Order Before Packing

1. Compare the staged goods with the authorized order.
2. Confirm that the goods selected for packing correspond to that order.
3. Do not proceed with release if the goods cannot be associated with the correct authorized order.

### 7.3 Pack the Goods

1. Pack the items appropriately for shipment.
2. Keep all packed goods associated with the same order together.
3. Continue to maintain clear separation between this order and other staged orders.
4. Ensure the packed shipment corresponds to the authorized order.

### 7.4 Prepare Shipment Documentation

1. Prepare the packing list for the packed order.
2. Prepare all required transport/release documentation.
3. Ensure the documentation is associated with the correct packed shipment.
4. Make the packed shipment and its documentation available for checking.

### 7.5 Verify the Shipment

1. Loading/checking personnel shall check the packed shipment against the dispatch documentation.
2. Confirm that the packed goods correspond to the authorized order.
3. Verify the outgoing quantity.
4. Complete the verification before custody is transferred to the transport representative, carrier, or recipient.
5. Do not release the shipment when the goods, quantity, order association, or documentation do not correspond.

### 7.6 Stage and Release the Shipment

1. Keep the verified shipment clearly separated and associated with its documentation while awaiting handover.
2. Following successful verification, prepare the shipment for loading or collection.
3. Release the shipment to the transport representative, carrier, or recipient.
4. Transfer custody only after the outgoing quantity has been verified.

### 7.7 Update Stock Records

1. Record the released goods in the stock records.
2. Ensure the stock records reflect the goods and quantities actually released.
3. Confirm that the stock update corresponds to the shipment handed over for transport.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling

### 8.1 Orders Are Not Clearly Separated

- Stop packing, checking, or release activity for the affected orders.
- Re-establish clear separation and correct order association before proceeding.

### 8.2 Goods Do Not Correspond to the Authorized Order

- Do not pack or release the nonconforming goods as part of the order.
- Keep the shipment in the staging area and clearly separated.
- Refer the discrepancy to the Warehouse or Store Manager.
- Resume processing only after the shipment corresponds to the authorized order.

### 8.3 Quantity Discrepancy

- Do not transfer custody if the outgoing quantity cannot be verified or does not match the dispatch documentation.
- Keep the shipment separated and withhold release.
- Refer the discrepancy to the Warehouse or Store Manager for resolution.
- Repeat the quantity verification before handover.

### 8.4 Documentation Discrepancy or Missing Documentation

- Do not release the shipment if the packing list or required transport/release documentation is missing, incorrect, or cannot be associated with the shipment.
- Correct or complete the documentation before repeating the shipment check.

### 8.5 Stock Record Discrepancy

- Ensure the stock records are corrected so they reflect the goods and quantities actually released.
- Refer unresolved discrepancies to the Warehouse or Store Manager.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria

The process is complete when all of the following conditions have been met:

- The correct goods have been packed appropriately.
- The packed goods correspond to the authorized order.
- The order remained clearly separated from other staged orders.
- The packing list and required transport/release documentation have been prepared.
- The shipment has been checked against the dispatch documentation.
- The outgoing quantity has been verified before transfer of custody.
- The verified shipment has been handed over for transport.
- Stock records accurately reflect the released goods.
