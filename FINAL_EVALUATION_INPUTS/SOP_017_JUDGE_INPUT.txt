FINAL SOP EVALUATION JUDGE PROMPT — VERSION 1.0

You are an evaluator of generated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
for logistics and warehouse processes.

Your task is to evaluate ONE candidate SOP strictly against the supplied
SOURCE SCENARIO using the supplied eight-criterion evaluation rubric.

============================================================
EVALUATION PRINCIPLES
============================================================

1. Treat the SOURCE SCENARIO as the sole factual reference.

2. Do not use external operational knowledge to impose requirements that
   are absent from the source scenario.

3. Do not reward the candidate SOP for adding plausible but unsupported
   operational details.

4. Do not penalize the candidate SOP for omitting information that was
   never provided in the source scenario.

5. Evaluate only the candidate SOP provided.

6. Do not infer, identify, or speculate about which language model
   generated the candidate SOP.

7. Score each criterion independently.

8. Base each score on concrete comparison between the SOURCE SCENARIO and
   the CANDIDATE SOP.

9. Do not reward verbosity, document length, sophisticated formatting,
   or writing style unless these materially improve the criterion being
   evaluated.

10. Reasonable paraphrasing is acceptable when the operational meaning
    of the source scenario is preserved.

11. Do not compare the candidate SOP with other candidate SOPs.

12. Do not calculate or provide an overall score or model ranking.


============================================================
C1 — COMPLETENESS
============================================================

Evaluate whether all operationally relevant information explicitly
contained in the source scenario is represented in the SOP, including
required activities, information, roles, conditions, and expected outcomes.

Do not penalize information that was not provided in the source scenario.


============================================================
C2 — CORRECT SEQUENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether procedural activities follow the operational dependencies
and sequence specified or implied by the source scenario.

Penalize contradictory, illogical, or operationally incorrect ordering.


============================================================
C3 — ROLE CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether responsibilities are clearly and consistently assigned
to roles provided in the source scenario.

Penalize ambiguous responsibility, incorrect role assignment, or
unsupported new roles when these materially affect the procedure.


============================================================
C4 — INPUT / OUTPUT CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether required inputs and information, as well as expected
outputs and results, are clearly identifiable and consistent with the
source scenario.


============================================================
C5 — EXCEPTION HANDLING
============================================================

Evaluate whether exceptions, deviations, abnormal conditions, and
decision points contained in the source scenario are correctly identified
and handled.

Do not penalize the SOP merely because the source scenario itself contains
few exceptions.


============================================================
C6 — CONSTRAINT ADHERENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether explicit constraints and decision conditions contained
in the source scenario are preserved and respected throughout the SOP.


============================================================
C7 — GROUNDEDNESS / NO HALLUCINATION
============================================================

Evaluate whether operational claims in the SOP remain grounded in the
source scenario.

Penalize invented systems, roles, requirements, thresholds, documents,
technologies, approvals, rules, or other material operational details
that are unsupported by the source scenario.

Do not penalize harmless paraphrasing, organizational formatting, or
wording changes that preserve the original operational meaning.


============================================================
C8 — USABILITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether the SOP is clear, structured, internally consistent,
unambiguous, and actionable for the intended warehouse or logistics user,
while remaining faithful to the source scenario.


============================================================
SCORING SCALE
============================================================

5 — FULLY SATISFIES
The SOP fully satisfies the criterion. No substantive deficiency is
identifiable.

4 — GOOD
The SOP satisfies the criterion with only a minor omission, ambiguity,
or weakness that does not materially affect the procedure.

3 — ADEQUATE
The SOP partially satisfies the criterion. One meaningful deficiency or
several minor deficiencies are present, but the SOP remains substantially
correct and usable on this dimension.

2 — POOR
Major deficiencies are present. Important information, logic, or
requirements are missing, unclear, or incorrect and materially affect
this dimension.

1 — VERY POOR
The SOP fundamentally fails the criterion, substantially contradicts the
source scenario, or omits or incorrectly handles most essential elements
relevant to the criterion.


============================================================
SCORING PROCEDURE
============================================================

For EACH of the eight criteria:

1. Compare the candidate SOP directly with the source scenario.
2. Identify relevant evidence.
3. Identify substantive omissions, contradictions, unsupported additions,
   or ambiguities relevant to that criterion.
4. Assign exactly one INTEGER score from 1 to 5.
5. Provide a concise justification explaining the score.

Evaluate all eight criteria even if deficiencies overlap.

Do not calculate the total score. The experimental evaluation script will
calculate totals after the individual criterion scores have been recorded.

SOURCE SCENARIO:

Process: Stock Transfer and Redistribution

Scenario:
Approved stock is transferred from one warehouse to another warehouse within the same logistics network.

Objective:
Prepare, release and document an authorized stock transfer while preserving inventory traceability.

Operational information:
An approved transfer request identifies the goods and quantities to be moved from the sending warehouse to another warehouse. The required goods are available. Warehouse personnel must reserve, pick and stage the correct goods and prepare the required release and transport documentation. The outgoing quantity must be removed from the sending warehouse records. The receiving warehouse will subsequently confirm receipt through its receiving process.

Roles involved:
Supply/Inventory Officer, Warehouse Manager, Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff, receiving warehouse personnel.

Constraints / decision conditions:
Stock may only be released against an approved transfer request. Requested items must remain clearly associated with the correct transfer during staging and loading. Inventory records must reflect the outgoing movement.

Expected outcome:
The correct goods are released to the destination warehouse with complete documentation and traceable stock records.


CANDIDATE SOP:

# Standard Operating Procedure: Stock Transfer and Redistribution

## 1. SOP Title
Standard Operating Procedure: Stock Transfer and Redistribution

## 2. Purpose
To prepare, release, and document an authorized stock transfer while preserving inventory traceability.

## 3. Scope
This procedure applies to the transfer of approved stock from one warehouse to another warehouse within the same logistics network.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
The following roles are involved in this process. Specific operational responsibilities are assigned based on the provided process information.

*   **Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff:** Responsible for reserving, picking, and staging the correct goods; preparing release and transport documentation; and ensuring outgoing quantities are removed from sending warehouse records.
*   **Receiving Warehouse Personnel:** Responsible for confirming receipt of goods through the receiving process at the destination warehouse.
*   **Supply/Inventory Officer:** Involved in the process as identified in the scenario. (Note: Specific operational tasks for this role are not detailed in the provided scenario information.)
*   **Warehouse Manager:** Involved in the process as identified in the scenario. (Note: Specific operational tasks for this role are not detailed in the provided scenario information.)

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
*   An approved transfer request identifying the goods and quantities to be moved.
*   Confirmation that the required goods are available at the sending warehouse.
*   Access to sending warehouse inventory records.

## 6. Expected Outputs
*   Correct goods released to the destination warehouse.
*   Complete release and transport documentation.
*   Traceable stock records reflecting the outgoing movement.
*   Receipt confirmation from the receiving warehouse.

## 7. Procedure

1.  **Verify Transfer Authorization**
    *   Obtain the approved transfer request.
    *   Review the request to identify the specific goods and quantities to be moved from the sending warehouse to the receiving warehouse.
    *   **Constraint:** Stock may only be released against an approved transfer request.

2.  **Verify Availability**
    *   Confirm that the required goods identified in the transfer request are available at the sending warehouse.

3.  **Reserve, Pick, and Stage Goods**
    *   Reserve the correct goods based on the transfer request.
    *   Pick the reserved goods from storage.
    *   Stage the goods for transport.
    *   **Constraint:** Requested items must remain clearly associated with the correct transfer during staging and loading.

4.  **Prepare Documentation**
    *   Prepare the required release and transport documentation for the shipment.

5.  **Update Inventory Records**
    *   Remove the outgoing quantity from the sending warehouse records.
    *   **Constraint:** Inventory records must reflect the outgoing movement.

6.  **Release Goods**
    *   Release the staged goods to the destination warehouse with the prepared documentation.

7.  **Confirm Receipt**
    *   The receiving warehouse personnel will confirm receipt of the goods through their receiving process.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
*   **Unapproved Request:** If a transfer request is not approved, stock must not be released.
*   **Loss of Association:** If requested items lose clear association with the correct transfer during staging or loading, the process must be halted until the association is restored to ensure traceability.
*   **Record Discrepancy:** If inventory records do not reflect the outgoing movement after release, corrective action must be taken to update the records to maintain traceability.
*   **Availability Issues:** If the required goods are not available despite the request, the transfer cannot proceed as described in the operational information.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The process is considered complete when:
*   The correct goods have been released to the destination warehouse.
*   Complete documentation accompanies the release.
*   Stock records are traceable and reflect the outgoing movement.
*   The receiving warehouse has confirmed receipt through its receiving process.
