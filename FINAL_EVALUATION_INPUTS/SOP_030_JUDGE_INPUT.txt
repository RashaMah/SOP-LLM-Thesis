FINAL SOP EVALUATION JUDGE PROMPT — VERSION 1.0

You are an evaluator of generated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
for logistics and warehouse processes.

Your task is to evaluate ONE candidate SOP strictly against the supplied
SOURCE SCENARIO using the supplied eight-criterion evaluation rubric.

============================================================
EVALUATION PRINCIPLES
============================================================

1. Treat the SOURCE SCENARIO as the sole factual reference.

2. Do not use external operational knowledge to impose requirements that
   are absent from the source scenario.

3. Do not reward the candidate SOP for adding plausible but unsupported
   operational details.

4. Do not penalize the candidate SOP for omitting information that was
   never provided in the source scenario.

5. Evaluate only the candidate SOP provided.

6. Do not infer, identify, or speculate about which language model
   generated the candidate SOP.

7. Score each criterion independently.

8. Base each score on concrete comparison between the SOURCE SCENARIO and
   the CANDIDATE SOP.

9. Do not reward verbosity, document length, sophisticated formatting,
   or writing style unless these materially improve the criterion being
   evaluated.

10. Reasonable paraphrasing is acceptable when the operational meaning
    of the source scenario is preserved.

11. Do not compare the candidate SOP with other candidate SOPs.

12. Do not calculate or provide an overall score or model ranking.


============================================================
C1 — COMPLETENESS
============================================================

Evaluate whether all operationally relevant information explicitly
contained in the source scenario is represented in the SOP, including
required activities, information, roles, conditions, and expected outcomes.

Do not penalize information that was not provided in the source scenario.


============================================================
C2 — CORRECT SEQUENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether procedural activities follow the operational dependencies
and sequence specified or implied by the source scenario.

Penalize contradictory, illogical, or operationally incorrect ordering.


============================================================
C3 — ROLE CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether responsibilities are clearly and consistently assigned
to roles provided in the source scenario.

Penalize ambiguous responsibility, incorrect role assignment, or
unsupported new roles when these materially affect the procedure.


============================================================
C4 — INPUT / OUTPUT CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether required inputs and information, as well as expected
outputs and results, are clearly identifiable and consistent with the
source scenario.


============================================================
C5 — EXCEPTION HANDLING
============================================================

Evaluate whether exceptions, deviations, abnormal conditions, and
decision points contained in the source scenario are correctly identified
and handled.

Do not penalize the SOP merely because the source scenario itself contains
few exceptions.


============================================================
C6 — CONSTRAINT ADHERENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether explicit constraints and decision conditions contained
in the source scenario are preserved and respected throughout the SOP.


============================================================
C7 — GROUNDEDNESS / NO HALLUCINATION
============================================================

Evaluate whether operational claims in the SOP remain grounded in the
source scenario.

Penalize invented systems, roles, requirements, thresholds, documents,
technologies, approvals, rules, or other material operational details
that are unsupported by the source scenario.

Do not penalize harmless paraphrasing, organizational formatting, or
wording changes that preserve the original operational meaning.


============================================================
C8 — USABILITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether the SOP is clear, structured, internally consistent,
unambiguous, and actionable for the intended warehouse or logistics user,
while remaining faithful to the source scenario.


============================================================
SCORING SCALE
============================================================

5 — FULLY SATISFIES
The SOP fully satisfies the criterion. No substantive deficiency is
identifiable.

4 — GOOD
The SOP satisfies the criterion with only a minor omission, ambiguity,
or weakness that does not materially affect the procedure.

3 — ADEQUATE
The SOP partially satisfies the criterion. One meaningful deficiency or
several minor deficiencies are present, but the SOP remains substantially
correct and usable on this dimension.

2 — POOR
Major deficiencies are present. Important information, logic, or
requirements are missing, unclear, or incorrect and materially affect
this dimension.

1 — VERY POOR
The SOP fundamentally fails the criterion, substantially contradicts the
source scenario, or omits or incorrectly handles most essential elements
relevant to the criterion.


============================================================
SCORING PROCEDURE
============================================================

For EACH of the eight criteria:

1. Compare the candidate SOP directly with the source scenario.
2. Identify relevant evidence.
3. Identify substantive omissions, contradictions, unsupported additions,
   or ambiguities relevant to that criterion.
4. Assign exactly one INTEGER score from 1 to 5.
5. Provide a concise justification explaining the score.

Evaluate all eight criteria even if deficiencies overlap.

Do not calculate the total score. The experimental evaluation script will
calculate totals after the individual criterion scores have been recorded.

MATERIAL TO EVALUATE
============================================================

SOURCE SCENARIO:

Process: Reverse Logistics and Damaged Goods Handling

Scenario:
Goods already held in the warehouse are identified as non-conforming and must be returned.

Objective:
Remove identified non-conforming goods from usable inventory and prepare them for controlled return.

Operational information:
A stored batch is identified as unsuitable for normal use because of a documented quality problem. Warehouse personnel must identify the affected quantity, remove it from normal usable stock, place it in a clearly separated holding area and update the relevant stock records. The responsible logistics/supply function must coordinate the further return or disposition process. Required supporting documentation must accompany the goods when they leave the warehouse.

Roles involved:
Supply Officer/Logistics function, Warehouse or Store Manager, Storekeeper, warehouse staff.

Constraints / decision conditions:
Affected goods must not remain available for normal picking. Their identity and quantity must remain traceable. The goods may only leave the warehouse through an authorized and documented process.

Expected outcome:
Affected stock is isolated, correctly recorded and prepared for authorized return without contaminating normal available inventory.


CANDIDATE SOP:

## 1. SOP Title
Reverse Logistics and Damaged Goods Handling Procedure

## 2. Purpose
The purpose of this Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is to outline the steps required to identify, isolate, and process non-conforming goods within the warehouse, ensuring they are removed from usable inventory and prepared for controlled return.

## 3. Scope
This SOP applies to all warehouse staff, Storekeepers, Warehouse or Store Managers, and Supply Officers/Logistics function involved in handling non-conforming goods within the warehouse.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Warehouse Staff**: Identify and segregate non-conforming goods, update stock records as directed.
- **Storekeeper**: Assist in identifying the affected quantity, physically segregate non-conforming goods, and ensure accurate stock record updates.
- **Warehouse or Store Manager**: Oversee the segregation process, ensure non-conforming goods are placed in a designated holding area, and verify stock record updates.
- **Supply Officer/Logistics Function**: Coordinate the further return or disposition process for non-conforming goods and ensure required supporting documentation is prepared.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
- Identification of non-conforming goods with documented quality problems.
- Accurate quantity of affected goods.
- Relevant stock records.

## 6. Expected Outputs
- Non-conforming goods isolated and held in a clearly separated area.
- Updated stock records reflecting the removal of non-conforming goods.
- Non-conforming goods prepared for authorized return with accompanying documentation.

## 7. Procedure
1. **Identification of Non-Conforming Goods**: Warehouse staff identify goods with documented quality problems.
2. **Quantification**: Determine the exact quantity of non-conforming goods.
3. **Segregation**: Warehouse staff, under the supervision of the Storekeeper, segregate the identified non-conforming goods from usable stock.
4. **Relocation to Holding Area**: Move the segregated goods to a clearly designated holding area within the warehouse, ensuring they are not available for normal picking.
5. **Update Stock Records**: The Storekeeper updates the relevant stock records to reflect the removal of non-conforming goods, ensuring their identity and quantity remain traceable.
6. **Verification**: The Warehouse or Store Manager verifies that non-conforming goods are correctly isolated and stock records are updated accurately.
7. **Coordination for Return**: The Supply Officer/Logistics function coordinates the return or disposition process, ensuring that the goods are accompanied by required supporting documentation when they leave the warehouse.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
- If the quantity of non-conforming goods cannot be accurately determined, the Warehouse or Store Manager must be notified to assess the situation and decide on the next steps.
- In cases where the designated holding area is full or unavailable, the Warehouse or Store Manager will identify an alternative holding area and ensure it is clearly designated for non-conforming goods.
- Any discrepancies in stock records or issues with the segregation process must be reported to the Warehouse or Store Manager for resolution.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The Reverse Logistics and Damaged Goods Handling process is considered complete when:
- Non-conforming goods are isolated in a designated holding area.
- Stock records have been accurately updated.
- The Supply Officer/Logistics function has coordinated the return or disposition process, and the goods are ready to leave the warehouse with the necessary documentation.
