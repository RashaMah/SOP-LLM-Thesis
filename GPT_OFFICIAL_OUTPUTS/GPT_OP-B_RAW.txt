# Standard Operating Procedure: Order Picking When Stock Is Unavailable at the Expected Location

## 1. SOP Title

**Order Picking — Requested Item or Quantity Not Physically Available at the Expected Picking Location**

## 2. Purpose

To ensure that a multi-item picking operation continues correctly when a requested item or quantity is unavailable at its listed picking location, without falsely confirming the missing quantity or selecting unauthorized substitute stock.

## 3. Scope

This SOP applies when:

- A picker is processing an authorized multi-item picking instruction.
- The picker reaches the listed location for a requested item.
- The required item or quantity is not physically available at that location.
- Other items listed in the picking instruction may remain available for picking.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### Picker/Storekeeper

- Follow the authorized picking instruction.
- Check the listed picking location for the requested item and quantity.
- Pick only items and quantities that are physically available and authorized.
- Do not falsely mark unavailable quantities as picked.
- Do not select an alternative batch or quantity unless warehouse stock rules permit it.
- Record the discrepancy between the physical stock and the inventory or picking information.
- Communicate the discrepancy to the responsible warehouse personnel.
- Continue picking other available items as appropriate.

### Storekeeper Responsible for Stock Records

- Receive the reported discrepancy.
- Check the relevant stock records.
- Reconcile the difference between the physical location and the inventory or picking information.
- Support back-order handling where applicable.

### Warehouse Supervisor/Manager

- Receive or oversee escalation of the discrepancy.
- Ensure the discrepancy is addressed through stock reconciliation or back-order handling, as applicable.
- Provide direction where the picker cannot proceed under the authorized instruction or warehouse stock rules.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information

- Authorized picking instruction.
- Requested item identification.
- Requested quantity.
- Listed picking location.
- Batch information, where included in the picking instruction.
- Physical quantity found at the listed location.
- Applicable warehouse stock rules concerning alternative batches or quantities.

## 6. Expected Outputs

- Available items are picked correctly according to the authorized picking instruction.
- Only quantities physically picked are confirmed as picked.
- Missing quantities remain unconfirmed.
- The discrepancy is recorded and communicated.
- Stock records are checked and reconciled.
- The unavailable quantity is escalated for stock reconciliation or back-order handling, as applicable.

## 7. Procedure

### 7.1 Follow the Picking Instruction

1. Use the authorized picking instruction to identify:
   - The requested item.
   - The required quantity.
   - The listed picking location.
   - Any specified batch information.
2. Go to the listed picking location.
3. Verify the physical availability of the requested item and quantity.

### 7.2 Identify the Discrepancy

1. If the required quantity is not physically available at the listed location, compare:
   - The requested quantity.
   - The quantity physically available.
2. Treat any difference between the physical stock and the inventory or picking information as a discrepancy.

### 7.3 Handle Available Quantity

1. Pick only the quantity that is physically available and authorized by the picking instruction and warehouse stock rules.
2. Do not record or confirm any unavailable quantity as picked.
3. Do not select a different batch or alternative quantity unless this is permitted by the warehouse stock rules.
4. If an alternative batch or quantity is not permitted, leave the missing quantity unpicked.

### 7.4 Continue the Multi-Item Pick

1. Continue processing the other items listed in the picking instruction when they are available.
2. Pick each available item according to the authorized instruction.
3. Keep the missing quantity identified as unpicked while allowing available items to continue through the appropriate process.

### 7.5 Record the Discrepancy

Record the discrepancy between the physical location and the inventory or picking information. The record must identify, using the information available from the picking instruction and physical check:

- The affected item.
- The listed picking location.
- The requested quantity.
- The quantity physically available.
- The resulting missing quantity.
- Any relevant batch information.

The scenario does not specify the method or format for recording the discrepancy. Use the established warehouse method without falsely confirming the missing stock.

### 7.6 Communicate and Escalate

1. Communicate the discrepancy to the storekeeper responsible for stock records.
2. Escalate the issue to the Warehouse Supervisor/Manager where stock reconciliation, back-order handling, or further direction is required.
3. Ensure the responsible warehouse personnel can check and reconcile the stock records against the physical quantity.

### 7.7 Address the Outstanding Quantity

1. Keep the unavailable quantity unconfirmed as picked.
2. Refer the unavailable quantity for:
   - Stock reconciliation; or
   - Back-order handling, as applicable.
3. Do not close the discrepancy by inventing stock, recording stock that was not physically picked, or using an unauthorized alternative.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling

### 8.1 No Stock Available at the Listed Location

- Record the full requested quantity as unavailable.
- Do not confirm any quantity as picked.
- Continue picking other available items.
- Communicate and escalate the discrepancy for reconciliation or back-order handling.

### 8.2 Partial Quantity Available

- Pick only the physically available quantity when authorized by the picking instruction and warehouse stock rules.
- Do not confirm the missing balance as picked.
- Record and communicate the shortage.

### 8.3 Alternative Batch or Quantity Identified

- Do not select the alternative batch or quantity unless warehouse stock rules permit it.
- If permission is not established, leave the quantity unpicked and escalate the discrepancy.
- The scenario does not specify how permission is obtained; follow the applicable warehouse stock rules.

### 8.4 Physical Stock and Records Do Not Match

- Do not change the physical result to match the inventory or picking information.
- Record the actual physical quantity found.
- Notify the storekeeper responsible for stock records.
- Escalate to the Warehouse Supervisor/Manager as required for reconciliation.

### 8.5 Uncertainty About How to Proceed

- Do not substitute stock or confirm unavailable quantities.
- Seek direction from the Warehouse Supervisor/Manager.
- Keep the affected quantity unpicked until it can be handled under the authorized picking instruction and warehouse stock rules.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria

The process is complete when:

- All physically available and authorized items have been picked correctly.
- No unavailable quantity has been falsely confirmed as picked.
- No unauthorized alternative batch or quantity has been selected.
- The discrepancy has been recorded.
- The discrepancy has been communicated to the responsible warehouse personnel.
- The missing quantity has been referred for stock reconciliation or back-order handling, as applicable.
