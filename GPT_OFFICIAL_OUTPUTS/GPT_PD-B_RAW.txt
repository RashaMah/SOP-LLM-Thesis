# SOP Title

**Packing and Dispatch Preparation for Large Outgoing Shipments**

## 1. Purpose

To ensure that a large approved shipment is correctly staged, packed, documented, loaded, verified, and released without:

- Mixing goods from different orders;
- Exceeding physical warehouse limitations;
- Exceeding vehicle weight or volume limitations; or
- Creating undocumented differences between loaded quantities and the dispatch/Waybill information.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to large approved outgoing shipments that have already been picked and require planned staging and loading rather than immediate release.

It covers:

- Staging-area organization;
- Shipment identification and separation;
- Packing;
- Preparation of dispatch documentation;
- Loading coordination;
- Quantity verification; and
- Release of the loaded vehicle.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Warehouse Manager

- Coordinate the staging and loading operation.
- Ensure warehouse physical and operational limitations are considered.
- Ensure vehicle weight and volume limitations are considered.
- Confirm that loading is limited to the authorized shipment.
- Coordinate the involved warehouse, checking/security, and transport personnel.
- Address or coordinate resolution of deviations before release.

### 3.2 Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff

- Organize the staging area.
- Move the picked shipment into the designated staging arrangement.
- Keep the shipment clearly identified.
- Prevent goods belonging to different orders from being mixed.
- Pack the shipment.
- Prepare the available packing and dispatch documentation, including dispatch/Waybill information.
- Load goods as coordinated and within applicable operational and vehicle constraints.

### 3.3 Tally/Checking or Security Personnel

- Check loaded quantities against the dispatch/Waybill information.
- Verify that the vehicle load corresponds to the authorized shipment and supporting documentation.
- Identify and report order mixing, quantity differences, or other discrepancies before release.

### 3.4 Transport Personnel

- Coordinate with the Warehouse Manager and warehouse personnel during loading.
- Provide or confirm the vehicle’s applicable weight and volume limitations.
- Ensure loading does not exceed vehicle capacity constraints.
- Participate in dispatch preparation and vehicle release activities as applicable.

## 4. Required Inputs / Information

The following must be available:

- An approved shipment that has been picked;
- Clear identification of the authorized shipment and its order;
- Picked goods intended for dispatch;
- Shipment quantities;
- Packing and dispatch information;
- Dispatch/Waybill information;
- Information on applicable vehicle weight and volume limitations;
- Information on relevant warehouse physical and operational limitations; and
- Availability of an appropriate staging area and coordinated loading operation.

## 5. Expected Outputs

- The complete authorized shipment is clearly identified and staged.
- Goods from different orders remain separated.
- The shipment is packed and prepared for dispatch.
- Packing and dispatch documentation is prepared.
- The vehicle is loaded without exceeding applicable weight, volume, warehouse, or operational constraints.
- Loaded quantities match the dispatch/Waybill information.
- Any quantity differences are resolved or documented before release.
- The verified shipment is released for dispatch.

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Confirm Shipment Readiness

1. Confirm that the outgoing shipment is approved and has been picked.
2. Confirm the authorized shipment identity and quantities using the available shipment and dispatch information.
3. Confirm that the shipment requires planned staging and loading because of its volume.
4. Coordinate the required warehouse, tally/checking or security, and transport personnel.

### 6.2 Assess Loading and Staging Constraints

1. Review the physical limitations of the warehouse that may affect staging, movement, or loading.
2. Review applicable operational capacity constraints.
3. Confirm the vehicle’s weight and volume limitations with the available information and transport personnel.
4. Plan the staging and loading activity so that these constraints are not exceeded.
5. Do not begin or continue loading if the planned load cannot be accommodated within the applicable constraints.

### 6.3 Organize the Staging Area

1. Prepare the staging area for the shipment.
2. Arrange the area so the shipment can remain clearly identified throughout staging and loading.
3. Keep goods belonging to different orders physically and operationally separate.
4. Ensure that the staging arrangement supports checking, packing, and controlled movement to the vehicle.

### 6.4 Stage and Identify the Shipment

1. Move the picked goods into the organized staging area.
2. Maintain clear identification of the shipment and associated order.
3. Verify during staging that goods from another order have not been included.
4. Keep the shipment separated from other orders until loading is complete.

### 6.5 Pack the Shipment

1. Pack the staged goods for dispatch.
2. Maintain order identification and separation during packing.
3. Ensure packed goods remain traceable to the authorized shipment.
4. Arrange packed goods so they can be checked and loaded without mixing orders.

The scenario does not specify packing materials, packing methods, or package-labeling requirements. These details must not be assumed under this SOP.

### 6.6 Prepare Dispatch Documentation

1. Prepare the available packing and dispatch documentation.
2. Ensure the dispatch/Waybill information corresponds to the authorized shipment.
3. Confirm that the documented quantities represent the goods authorized for loading.
4. Keep the documentation available for checking before vehicle release.

### 6.7 Coordinate and Perform Loading

1. Coordinate the loading sequence among the Warehouse Manager, warehouse staff, checking/security personnel, and transport personnel.
2. Move only goods belonging to the authorized shipment from the staging area to the vehicle.
3. Maintain clear order identification and separation throughout loading.
4. Monitor the load against:
   - Vehicle weight limitations;
   - Vehicle volume limitations;
   - Warehouse physical limitations; and
   - Operational capacity constraints.
5. Stop loading if:
   - Goods from another order are identified;
   - A capacity or operational constraint may be exceeded;
   - Shipment identity cannot be confirmed; or
   - The goods being loaded do not correspond to the authorized shipment or documentation.

### 6.8 Verify the Loaded Shipment

1. Tally/checking or security personnel must compare the quantities loaded against the dispatch/Waybill information.
2. Verify that:
   - The load corresponds to the authorized shipment;
   - No goods from another order are included;
   - Loaded quantities match the documented quantities; and
   - Applicable vehicle and operational constraints have been respected.
3. Identify any quantity difference or documentation mismatch before release.
4. Do not release the vehicle while an undocumented quantity difference remains.

### 6.9 Release the Shipment

1. Confirm that loading and verification are complete.
2. Confirm that the loaded goods correspond to the authorized shipment and dispatch/Waybill information.
3. Confirm that no order mixing has occurred.
4. Confirm that no unresolved or undocumented quantity differences remain.
5. Confirm that vehicle weight/volume and warehouse operational constraints have not been exceeded.
6. Release the shipment only after all required checks are complete.

## 7. Exception and Deviation Handling

### 7.1 Mixed or Suspected Mixed Orders

If goods from different orders are found or suspected to be mixed:

1. Stop the affected staging or loading activity.
2. Isolate and re-identify the affected goods.
3. Check the goods against the authorized shipment information.
4. Remove goods that do not belong to the authorized shipment.
5. Recheck the corrected shipment before loading continues or release occurs.

### 7.2 Quantity Difference

If loaded quantities do not match the dispatch/Waybill information:

1. Stop release of the shipment.
2. Recheck the staged, packed, and loaded quantities.
3. Compare the physical quantities with the authorized shipment and documentation.
4. Correct the physical load or the documentation, as applicable to the authorized shipment.
5. Ensure any remaining quantity difference is documented.
6. Repeat the quantity check before release.

The scenario does not specify who may authorize documentation changes or shipment quantity changes. No such approval authority is established by this SOP.

### 7.3 Vehicle Capacity Constraint

If the shipment would exceed the vehicle’s weight or volume limitation:

1. Stop loading before the limitation is exceeded.
2. Do not release an overloaded vehicle.
3. Refer the issue to the Warehouse Manager and transport personnel for coordination.
4. Resume loading or release only when the load is within the applicable vehicle constraints and corresponds to the authorized shipment and documentation.

The scenario does not specify whether another vehicle, a split shipment, or another transport arrangement may be used. Such action must not be assumed.

### 7.4 Warehouse or Operational Constraint

If a warehouse physical limitation or operational constraint prevents safe or controlled staging or loading:

1. Stop the affected activity.
2. Maintain shipment identification and order separation.
3. Notify the Warehouse Manager.
4. Reorganize the activity within the available limitations before continuing.
5. Do not proceed if order separation, verification, or capacity compliance cannot be maintained.

### 7.5 Documentation Mismatch or Missing Information

If the load cannot be confirmed against the authorized shipment or dispatch/Waybill information:

1. Stop loading or release, as applicable.
2. Maintain the shipment in an identified and separated condition.
3. Reconcile the physical goods with the available shipment documentation.
4. Proceed only when the authorized shipment and quantities can be verified.

## 8. Process Completion Criteria

The process is complete only when all of the following conditions are met:

- The complete authorized shipment has been staged, packed, and loaded.
- The shipment remained clearly identified throughout the process.
- Goods belonging to different orders were not mixed.
- Packing and dispatch documentation has been prepared.
- The vehicle load corresponds to the authorized shipment and documentation.
- Loaded quantities have been checked against the dispatch/Waybill information.
- No unresolved or undocumented quantity differences remain.
- Vehicle weight and volume limitations have not been exceeded.
- Warehouse physical and operational constraints have been respected.
- Verification is complete and the shipment has been released for dispatch.
