**1. SOP Title**
Goods Receipt and Verification Procedure for Scheduled Supplier Deliveries

**2. Purpose**
To ensure that goods received from suppliers are correctly verified, documented, and stored in the warehouse, and that inventory records are accurately updated.

**3. Scope**
This SOP applies to all scheduled supplier deliveries received at the warehouse with valid purchase-order references, shipping documentation, and packing lists.

**4. Roles and Responsibilities**
- **Warehouse Manager/Store Manager**: Oversees the goods receipt process, ensures compliance with this SOP, and manages warehouse staff.
- **Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff**: Responsible for unloading goods, verifying shipments against documentation, inspecting goods, and updating inventory records.
- **Receiving or Inspection Personnel**: Conducts the initial verification and inspection of received goods.

**5. Required Inputs / Information**
- Valid purchase order reference
- Shipping documentation
- Packing list
- Goods delivered by the supplier

**6. Expected Outputs**
- Verified shipment details
- Receiving record documentation
- Updated warehouse stock records
- Goods stored in the designated storage area

**7. Procedure**
1. Upon arrival of a scheduled supplier delivery, the Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff checks for the presence of shipping documentation and a packing list.
2. The receiving personnel verifies the shipment documentation against the expected purchase order reference to ensure it matches and is valid.
3. The goods are unloaded and visually inspected for damage or discrepancies.
4. The Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff compares the delivered items and quantities with the details on the packing list and shipping documentation.
5. If the delivered goods match the documentation in terms of identity and quantity, and are found to be in satisfactory condition upon visual inspection, they are accepted.
6. The receiving personnel documents the receipt by creating a receiving record, noting any observations or discrepancies.
7. Accepted goods are moved to the designated storage area.
8. The Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff updates the warehouse stock records to reflect the new inventory.

**8. Exception and Deviation Handling**
- If the shipment lacks valid documentation or there are discrepancies between the goods received and the documentation, the goods are not accepted into stock until the issue is resolved.
- In cases of damaged goods or discrepancies, the receiving personnel documents the issue in the receiving record and notifies the Warehouse Manager/Store Manager.
- The Warehouse Manager/Store Manager is responsible for resolving discrepancies or damaged goods issues with the supplier.

**9. Process Completion Criteria**
The goods receipt process is considered complete when:
- The shipment has been verified against the documentation.
- The goods have been inspected and accepted.
- A receiving record has been created.
- The goods have been stored in the designated storage area.
- Warehouse stock records have been updated to reflect the receipt of goods.
