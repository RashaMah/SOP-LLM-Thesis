1. SOP Title
Packing and Dispatch Preparation for Large Shipments

2. Purpose
To ensure that large shipments are prepared and dispatched efficiently and accurately, without mixing orders or exceeding operational constraints.

3. Scope
This SOP applies to the preparation and dispatch of large shipments that require coordinated staging and loading within the warehouse.

4. Roles and Responsibilities
- Warehouse Manager: Oversees the entire process, ensures compliance with operational constraints, and coordinates between different roles.
- Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff: Responsible for organizing the staging area, staging the shipment, and preparing packing and dispatch documentation.
- Tally/Checking or Security Personnel: Verify the shipment against the dispatch/Waybill information before and after loading.
- Transport Personnel: Load the shipment onto the vehicle and ensure it is secured properly.

5. Required Inputs / Information
- Approved shipment details
- Dispatch/Waybill information
- Vehicle capacity details (weight and volume)
- Physical warehouse limitations

6. Expected Outputs
- Staged and packed shipment
- Accurate packing and dispatch documentation
- Loaded vehicle corresponding to the authorized shipment and documentation
- Verified shipment quantities matching the dispatch/Waybill information

7. Procedure
1. Upon notification of a large approved shipment, the Warehouse Manager coordinates with the Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff to organize the staging area.
2. The Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff stages the shipment in the designated area, ensuring it is clearly identified and separated from other orders.
3. The Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff prepares the packing and dispatch documentation.
4. The Warehouse Manager reviews the vehicle capacity details and physical warehouse limitations to plan the loading operation.
5. The Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff and Tally/Checking or Security Personnel verify the staged shipment against the dispatch/Waybill information.
6. Transport Personnel, under the supervision of the Warehouse Manager or Tally/Checking or Security Personnel, load the shipment onto the vehicle, respecting vehicle weight and volume limitations.
7. During loading, the Tally/Checking or Security Personnel check the quantities being loaded against the dispatch/Waybill information.
8. Upon completion of loading, the Tally/Checking or Security Personnel verify that the loaded quantities match the dispatch/Waybill information.
9. The Warehouse Manager or designated personnel confirm that the vehicle load corresponds to the authorized shipment and documentation before release.

8. Exception and Deviation Handling
- If a discrepancy is found between the staged shipment and the dispatch/Waybill information, the loading operation is halted until the discrepancy is resolved.
- If the vehicle capacity is insufficient for the shipment, the Warehouse Manager is notified, and alternative arrangements are made.
- In case of mixing of goods from different orders during staging or loading, the Warehouse Manager is immediately informed, and the affected shipments are re-staged and re-verified.

9. Process Completion Criteria
The process is considered complete when:
- The entire authorized shipment is staged and packed.
- The shipment is loaded onto the vehicle.
- The loaded quantities are verified to match the dispatch/Waybill information.
- The vehicle load is confirmed to correspond to the authorized shipment and documentation.
- The shipment is released for dispatch.
