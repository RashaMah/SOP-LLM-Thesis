1. SOP Title
Packing and Dispatch Preparation SOP

2. Purpose
To ensure that picked orders are accurately packed, documented, and released for transport, maintaining the integrity of the shipment and updating stock records accordingly.

3. Scope
This SOP applies to all warehouse staff involved in the packing and dispatch preparation of picked orders, including Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper, Warehouse or Store Manager, loading/checking personnel, and interactions with transport representatives.

4. Roles and Responsibilities
- Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper: Responsible for packing items, preparing packing lists, and ensuring that goods are correctly associated with their respective orders.
- Warehouse or Store Manager: Oversees the packing and dispatch process, ensuring compliance with this SOP.
- Loading/Checking Personnel: Verifies the packed goods against the dispatch documentation before releasing them to the transport representative.
- Transport Representative: Receives the prepared shipment and takes custody of it for transport.

5. Required Inputs / Information
- Completed picking order in the staging area.
- Order details (including items and quantities).
- Dispatch documentation requirements.

6. Expected Outputs
- Packed goods corresponding to the authorized order.
- Prepared packing list.
- Required transport/release documentation.
- Updated stock records reflecting the released goods.

7. Procedure
1. **Packing**:
   - Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper retrieves the completed picking order from the staging area.
   - Ensures that items are packed according to the order details, maintaining order integrity.
   - Prepares a packing list detailing the items packed.

2. **Documentation**:
   - Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper prepares the required transport/release documentation.

3. **Verification**:
   - Loading/Checking Personnel checks the packed goods against the packing list and dispatch documentation to ensure accuracy and completeness.

4. **Release for Transport**:
   - Upon successful verification, the shipment is released to the transport representative.
   - The transport representative takes custody of the shipment.

5. **Stock Records Update**:
   - Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper or designated personnel update stock records to reflect the goods released.

8. Exception and Deviation Handling
- If discrepancies are found during verification (e.g., incorrect items or quantities), the shipment is not released. The discrepancy is investigated and rectified by the Warehouse Staff/Storekeeper under the supervision of the Warehouse or Store Manager before proceeding.
- If the transport representative refuses to take custody due to discrepancies or damage, the issue is resolved by the Warehouse or Store Manager in coordination with the transport representative.

9. Process Completion Criteria
The packing and dispatch preparation process is considered complete when:
- The goods are packed and correspond to the authorized order.
- The packing list and required transport/release documentation are prepared and accurate.
- The shipment is verified against the dispatch documentation.
- The shipment is released to the transport representative.
- Stock records are updated to reflect the released goods.
