## 1. SOP Title
Order Picking Procedure for Handling Unavailable Items

## 2. Purpose
To ensure that the order picking operation continues correctly when an item is not physically available at its expected picking location, and to maintain accurate stock records.

## 3. Scope
This SOP applies to all order picking operations within the warehouse where the picker encounters an item that is not available at its designated picking location.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Picker/Storekeeper**: Responsible for executing the picking instruction, identifying discrepancies between the physical stock and the picking instruction, and reporting these discrepancies.
- **Storekeeper responsible for stock records**: Responsible for checking and reconciling stock records upon notification of discrepancies.
- **Warehouse Supervisor/Manager**: Oversees the picking operation and ensures that discrepancies are addressed appropriately.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
- Authorized picking instruction
- Current stock records (for reference during discrepancy reporting and reconciliation)

## 6. Expected Outputs
- Accurate picking of available items as per the picking instruction
- Record of discrepancies between the physical stock and the picking instruction
- Notification to the Storekeeper responsible for stock records regarding discrepancies

## 7. Procedure
1. The Picker/Storekeeper receives the authorized picking instruction and begins the picking operation.
2. For each item listed on the picking instruction, the Picker/Storekeeper checks the designated picking location.
3. If an item is not found at its designated location or the required quantity is not available:
   - Do not pick or confirm the unavailable quantity.
   - Record the discrepancy, including the item details and the quantity missing.
   - Continue picking the other items listed on the picking instruction if they are available.
4. Upon completing the picking operation, the Picker/Storekeeper reports the discrepancy to the Storekeeper responsible for stock records.
5. The Storekeeper responsible for stock records checks and reconciles the stock records based on the discrepancy report.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
- If an item is not available at its designated picking location, the Picker/Storekeeper must not substitute it with another item or quantity unless permitted by the warehouse stock rules.
- The discrepancy must be recorded and communicated to the Storekeeper responsible for stock records for further action, which may include stock reconciliation or back-order handling.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The order picking process is considered complete when:
- All available items have been picked according to the picking instruction.
- Discrepancies have been recorded and reported to the Storekeeper responsible for stock records.
- The picking operation has been executed without falsely confirming unavailable quantities as picked.
