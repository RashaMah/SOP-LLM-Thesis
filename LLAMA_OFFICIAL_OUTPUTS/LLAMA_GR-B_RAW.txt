**1. SOP Title**
Goods Receipt Process for Handling Damaged Goods and Quantity Discrepancies

**2. Purpose**
To outline the procedure for processing an incoming shipment that contains damaged goods and quantity discrepancies, ensuring that discrepancies are correctly documented and communicated to the responsible logistics/supply function.

**3. Scope**
This SOP applies to all incoming shipments received by the warehouse that are found to have damaged goods or quantity discrepancies during the receiving process.

**4. Roles and Responsibilities**
- **Warehouse Manager/Store Manager**: Oversees the receiving process and ensures that discrepancies are handled according to this SOP.
- **Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff**: Responsible for unloading shipments, inspecting goods, and identifying discrepancies.
- **Inspection Personnel**: Conducts detailed inspections of goods to identify damage or discrepancies.
- **Responsible Supply/Logistics Function**: Receives notification of discrepancies and makes decisions regarding the disposition of damaged or discrepant goods.

**5. Required Inputs / Information**
- Incoming shipment with valid shipping documentation.
- Relevant order documentation (e.g., purchase order).
- Packing documentation (e.g., packing list).

**6. Expected Outputs**
- Accurate inventory records reflecting only accepted quantities and status.
- Documentation of discrepancies (damage and quantity).
- Notification to the responsible Supply/Logistics function regarding discrepancies.

**7. Procedure**
1. **Receipt and Inspection**:
   - Upon arrival, verify the shipment against the shipping documentation.
   - Unload the shipment and inspect the goods for damage or discrepancies.
   - Compare the physical delivery with the order and packing documentation to identify any mismatches.

2. **Identifying and Documenting Discrepancies**:
   - Identify and document any damaged goods or quantity discrepancies.
   - Clearly identify and separate damaged or questionable goods from normal stock.

3. **Processing Accepted Goods**:
   - Process goods that are not damaged or discrepant as normal stock, updating inventory records accordingly.

4. **Handling Discrepancies**:
   - Document all discrepancies (damage and quantity) in detail.
   - Inform the Warehouse Manager/Store Manager of the discrepancies.

5. **Notification and Escalation**:
   - The Warehouse Manager/Store Manager notifies the responsible Supply/Logistics function about the discrepancies, providing all relevant documentation.

6. **Decision on Discrepant Goods**:
   - Await decision from the responsible Supply/Logistics function regarding the disposition of damaged or discrepant goods.
   - Do not treat affected items as normal usable stock until a decision has been made.

7. **Updating Inventory Records**:
   - Update inventory records to reflect the accepted quantities and status of goods.

**8. Exception and Deviation Handling**
- In cases where the discrepancy cannot be resolved through the normal process, the Warehouse Manager/Store Manager will escalate the issue to the responsible Supply/Logistics function for further action.
- Any deviation from this SOP must be approved by the Warehouse Manager/Store Manager and documented.

**9. Process Completion Criteria**
The Goods Receipt process is considered complete when:
- All goods have been inspected and discrepancies documented.
- Inventory records have been updated to reflect the accepted quantities and status.
- The responsible Supply/Logistics function has been notified of discrepancies and has made a decision regarding the disposition of damaged or discrepant goods.
- Discrepant goods have been handled according to the decision made by the responsible Supply/Logistics function.
