## 1. SOP Title
Inventory Replenishment and Stock Realignment Procedure

## 2. Purpose
To detect inventory shortages, verify available stock, and initiate appropriate replenishment or stock realignment actions to meet known future demand without promising unavailable stock.

## 3. Scope
This SOP applies to all warehouse operations where inventory levels are reviewed against known future demand, and replenishment or stock realignment actions are required.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Inventory/Supply Officer**: Responsible for reviewing inventory levels, identifying shortages, and coordinating replenishment or stock realignment actions.
- **Warehouse Manager**: Oversees the verification of available stock and coordinates with the Storekeeper for stock checks.
- **Storekeeper**: Responsible for verifying the physical stock levels as per the inventory records.
- **Relevant Planning or Logistics Function**: Receives notifications of inventory shortages and is responsible for processing replenishment or stock realignment requests.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
- Current inventory levels for all items.
- Planned releases and additional known demand.
- Inventory requirements for planned releases.
- Information on stock availability at other locations (if applicable).

## 6. Expected Outputs
- Identification and documentation of inventory shortages.
- Notification to relevant stakeholders (Inventory/Supply Officer, Warehouse Manager, and relevant planning or logistics function) about the shortages.
- Initiation of either replenishment or stock realignment actions.

## 7. Procedure
1. **Review Inventory Levels**: The Inventory/Supply Officer reviews current inventory levels against the quantity required for planned releases and additional known demand to identify potential shortages.
   
2. **Identify Shortages**: If the available quantity is lower than the required quantity for an item, the Inventory/Supply Officer identifies it as a shortage.

3. **Verify Available Stock**: The Warehouse Manager instructs the Storekeeper to verify the physical stock level of the identified item(s) against the recorded inventory level.

4. **Confirm Shortage**: After verification by the Storekeeper, if a shortage is confirmed, the Warehouse Manager notifies the Inventory/Supply Officer.

5. **Check for Stock Realignment**: The Inventory/Supply Officer checks if stock is available at other locations that can be realigned to meet the shortage.

6. **Initiate Stock Realignment or Replenishment**:
   - If stock is available at another location, the Inventory/Supply Officer coordinates the transfer with the relevant logistics function.
   - If stock is not available at another location, the Inventory/Supply Officer initiates the replenishment process through the appropriate supply process.

7. **Notify Stakeholders**: The Inventory/Supply Officer notifies all relevant stakeholders (including the relevant planning or logistics function) about the shortage and the actions being taken.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
- **Inaccurate Inventory Records**: If during verification, discrepancies are found between physical stock and recorded inventory levels, the Storekeeper and Warehouse Manager should investigate and correct the records accordingly before proceeding.
- **Unavailability of Stock for Realignment or Replenishment**: If stock cannot be realigned or replenished in time to meet the demand, the Inventory/Supply Officer should notify the relevant planning or logistics function to adjust plans accordingly.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The Inventory Replenishment and Stock Realignment process is considered complete when:
- The inventory shortage is identified and documented.
- The available stock is verified.
- Either stock realignment or replenishment action is initiated.
- All relevant stakeholders are notified about the shortage and the actions taken.
