## 1. SOP Title
Reverse Logistics and Damaged Goods Handling for Product Recall

## 2. Purpose
The purpose of this Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is to outline the steps required to identify, isolate, and return all warehouse stock affected by a product recall due to quality or safety concerns, ensuring that the affected products are prevented from being issued and are handled in a controlled manner.

## 3. Scope
This SOP applies to all warehouse personnel involved in handling products that are subject to a recall notice. It covers the procedures for identifying, segregating, and dispatching affected stock.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Supply Officer**: Receives the recall notification and initiates the recall process.
- **Store Manager**: Oversees the overall recall process within the warehouse, ensuring compliance with this SOP.
- **Store Supervisor**: Coordinates the identification, segregation, and documentation of affected stock.
- **Storekeeper**: Responsible for updating stock records and ensuring that affected stock is properly marked and segregated.
- **Store Helper/Warehouse Staff**: Assists in the physical identification, segregation, and handling of affected stock.
- **Security**: Ensures that the dispatch of recalled goods is done with appropriate security and release controls.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information

- Formal recall notice received by the Supply function.
- Details of the affected product, including product identification and quantities.
- Current stock records.

## 6. Expected Outputs

- Affected stock identified and isolated.
- Stock records updated to reflect the status of the affected stock.
- Affected stock properly marked and segregated.
- Controlled dispatch of recalled goods with appropriate documentation and security/release controls.

## 7. Procedure

1. **Receipt of Recall Notice**: The Supply Officer receives a formal recall notice for a product currently stored in the warehouse and notifies the Store Manager.
   
2. **Identification of Affected Stock**: The Store Supervisor, assisted by the Storekeeper and Store Helper/Warehouse Staff, identifies all quantities of the affected product in the warehouse stock using the details provided in the recall notice.

3. **Isolation of Affected Stock**: Immediately after identification, the Store Supervisor ensures that the affected stock is stopped from being issued and is segregated in a separate, designated area within the warehouse.

4. **Marking of Affected Stock**: The Storekeeper ensures that the segregated stock is appropriately marked to indicate that it is subject to a recall and should not be used or issued.

5. **Update of Stock Records**: The Storekeeper updates the stock records to reflect that the affected quantities have been identified, isolated, and are not available for issue.

6. **Coordination for Dispatch**: The Store Manager coordinates with relevant offices and external parties to arrange for the dispatch of the recalled goods.

7. **Preparation for Dispatch**: The Store Supervisor ensures that the affected stock is prepared for dispatch, including the necessary packaging and documentation.

8. **Dispatch of Recalled Goods**: Under the supervision of the Store Supervisor and with the involvement of Security, the recalled goods are dispatched with appropriate documentation and security/release controls.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling

- If the affected stock cannot be immediately identified or segregated, the Store Supervisor must notify the Store Manager, who will coordinate for additional resources or support as necessary.
- In case of discrepancies in stock records or issues with the segregation process, the Storekeeper must document the discrepancies and notify the Store Supervisor for further action.
- Any security breaches or issues during the dispatch of recalled goods must be reported to the Security team and the Store Manager for immediate resolution.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria

The reverse logistics and damaged goods handling process for product recall is considered complete when:

- All affected stock has been identified and isolated.
- Stock records have been updated.
- Affected stock has been properly marked and segregated.
- Recalled goods have been dispatched with appropriate documentation and security/release controls.
- All relevant parties have been notified, and necessary documentation has been completed.
