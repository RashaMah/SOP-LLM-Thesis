# SOP: Product Recall Handling and Reverse Logistics for Damaged/Affected Goods

## 1. SOP Title
Standard Operating Procedure for Identification, Isolation, and Return of Recalled Warehouse Stock

## 2. Purpose
This SOP defines the procedure for identifying, blocking, segregating, recording, and returning warehouse stock affected by a formal product recall notice. It ensures that affected stock is immediately prevented from issue, remains traceable throughout the process, and is dispatched only under controlled and coordinated conditions.

## 3. Scope
This procedure applies to all warehouse stock affected by a formal recall notice due to a quality or safety concern. It covers activities from receipt of the recall notification through to controlled dispatch of the affected goods for return. It applies to all personnel involved in warehouse stock handling, security, and coordination with relevant offices and external parties.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Supply Officer** | Receives the formal recall notification; communicates recall details to warehouse management; coordinates with relevant offices and external parties prior to dispatch. |
| **Store Manager** | Oversees the overall recall response within the warehouse; ensures affected stock is blocked, segregated, and recorded; authorizes and oversees final dispatch. |
| **Store Supervisor** | Directs identification and physical segregation of affected stock; supervises marking and record updates; verifies completeness of the isolation process. |
| **Storekeeper** | Locates affected stock in warehouse records and physical storage; updates stock records to reflect blocked/segregated status; maintains traceability documentation. |
| **Store Helper / Warehouse Staff** | Physically moves and relocates affected stock to the segregated area under supervision; applies markings/labels to identify recalled goods. |
| **Security** | Enforces release controls; verifies documentation before allowing affected stock to leave warehouse premises during dispatch. |

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
- Formal recall notice (received by the Supply function) identifying the affected product due to a quality or safety concern.
- Warehouse stock records showing quantities and locations of the affected product.
- Coordination information/instructions from relevant offices and external parties regarding the return process.
- Documentation required for dispatch and release (as specified by applicable coordination requirements; specific document types are not detailed in this scenario).

## 6. Expected Outputs
- Full identification of all warehouse quantities of the affected product.
- Affected stock blocked from normal issue/release.
- Affected stock physically segregated in a separate, clearly marked area.
- Updated stock records reflecting the recall status and location of affected goods.
- Confirmed coordination with relevant offices and external parties prior to dispatch.
- Controlled dispatch of affected goods for return, with maintained traceability throughout.

## 7. Procedure

### Step 1: Receipt of Recall Notification
1.1 The **Supply Officer** receives the formal recall notice concerning a product due to a quality or safety concern.
1.2 The Supply Officer communicates the recall details (product identity and reason for recall) to the **Store Manager** without delay.

### Step 2: Immediate Block on Affected Stock
2.1 Upon confirmation of the recall, the **Store Manager** instructs the **Store Supervisor** to immediately prevent any release or issue of the affected product from warehouse stock.
2.2 The **Storekeeper** places a hold on the affected stock in the stock records so that it cannot be issued through normal warehouse processes.
2.3 This blocking action must occur immediately after the recall is confirmed, ahead of any physical segregation activity.

### Step 3: Identification of Affected Stock
3.1 The **Storekeeper**, under the direction of the **Store Supervisor**, searches warehouse stock records to identify all locations and quantities of the affected product.
3.2 The **Store Supervisor** verifies the identified quantities against physical stock in the warehouse to confirm completeness of identification.

### Step 4: Physical Segregation and Marking
4.1 The **Store Supervisor** directs **Store Helper/Warehouse Staff** to physically move all identified affected quantities to a separate, designated segregation area within the warehouse.
4.2 **Store Helper/Warehouse Staff** apply clear markings/labels to the segregated stock to identify it as recalled/affected goods and prevent accidental mixing with usable stock.
4.3 The **Store Supervisor** confirms that all identified quantities have been relocated and marked appropriately.

### Step 5: Stock Record Update
5.1 The **Storekeeper** updates stock records to reflect the new status and location of the affected goods (i.e., segregated/blocked recall stock).
5.2 All quantities moved out of usable stock must remain traceable in the records at all times, from the point of segregation through to final dispatch.

### Step 6: Coordination Prior to Dispatch
6.1 The **Supply Officer** coordinates with relevant offices and external parties regarding the recall return process before any dispatch of goods occurs.
6.2 The **Store Manager** ensures that all necessary coordination has been completed and confirmed before authorizing dispatch.

### Step 7: Controlled Dispatch for Recall Return
7.1 The **Store Manager** authorizes dispatch of the segregated affected stock only once coordination with relevant offices and external parties is confirmed and appropriate documentation is in place.
7.2 **Security** verifies the required documentation and applies release controls before allowing the affected stock to leave the warehouse premises.
7.3 The **Storekeeper** records the dispatch in stock records, maintaining full traceability of the quantities removed from the warehouse.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
- **Incomplete identification of affected stock:** If discrepancies are found between stock records and physical stock during verification (Step 3.2), the Store Supervisor must investigate the discrepancy before proceeding to segregation. The scenario does not specify a defined resolution procedure for such discrepancies; personnel should escalate to the Store Manager for guidance.
- **Attempted release of blocked stock:** If any attempt is made to issue stock that has been blocked under Step 2, the Storekeeper and Store Supervisor must immediately halt the transaction and confirm the block status in the records.
- **Missing or incomplete dispatch documentation:** Security must not release affected stock for dispatch if appropriate documentation and release controls are not in place, in accordance with the dispatch requirement stated in the constraints. Any such case must be referred back to the Store Manager and Supply Officer for resolution before dispatch proceeds.
- **Incomplete coordination with relevant offices/external parties:** Dispatch must not occur until coordination has been confirmed as required. If coordination is pending, the Store Manager must hold the segregated stock in the designated area until confirmation is received.
- **Traceability gaps:** If any affected quantity cannot be traced within stock records at any point in the process, the Storekeeper must immediately notify the Store Supervisor and Store Manager to investigate and correct the record before proceeding further.

*Note: This scenario does not specify exact documentation types, approval timelines, or escalation contacts beyond the roles listed. Where such details are required operationally, they must be defined through applicable organizational procedures not covered in this SOP.*

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The recall handling process is considered complete when:
- All warehouse quantities of the affected product have been identified.
- All identified quantities have been blocked from normal issue and physically segregated in the designated area with appropriate markings.
- Stock records have been fully updated to reflect the recall status and traceability of affected goods.
- Coordination with relevant offices and external parties has been completed and confirmed.
- All affected stock has been dispatched under controlled conditions, with appropriate documentation and security release controls applied, and traceability maintained throughout.
