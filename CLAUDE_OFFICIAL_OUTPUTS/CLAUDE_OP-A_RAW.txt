# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. SOP Title
**Standard Operating Procedure for Multi-Item Order Picking in the Warehouse**

## 2. Purpose
This SOP defines the standard process for accurately picking a normal multi-item picking wave from designated warehouse storage locations, in accordance with an approved picking instruction, and transferring the completed pick to the staging/packing area for the next fulfilment stage. The objective is to ensure picking accuracy, maintain inventory/location record integrity, and enable a smooth handover to subsequent order-fulfilment activities.

## 3. Scope
This procedure applies to:
- All normal multi-item picking waves involving multiple product references, required quantities, and multiple storage locations within the warehouse.
- The activities performed from receipt of an approved picking instruction through to transfer of the completed pick to the staging/packing area.

This procedure does not cover activities occurring after transfer to the staging/packing area (e.g., packing, dispatch), nor does it define specific software systems, technologies, or numerical thresholds, as these are not specified in the source information.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

**Storekeeper/Picker**
- Receives and reviews the approved picking instruction.
- Physically travels to each designated storage location and picks the specified product references and quantities.
- Checks each picked line against the picking instruction to confirm accuracy.
- Ensures inventory/location records remain accurate following physical movement of goods.
- Transfers the completed pick to the designated staging/packing area.
- Reports any discrepancies, shortages, or issues encountered during picking to the Warehouse Supervisor or Manager.

**Warehouse Supervisor or Manager**
- Assigns the picking task (order/wave) to one operator or picking team.
- Acts as point of escalation for exceptions or deviations identified during picking.
- Reviews and resolves reported discrepancies (e.g., missing items, quantity mismatches, location errors).
- Oversees that the picking process is carried out according to the authorized picking instruction.

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
- An approved picking instruction, containing:
  - Multiple product references
  - Required quantities for each product reference
  - Corresponding storage locations for each item
- Assignment of the picking task to a specific operator or picking team.
- Identified storage location information within the warehouse (as items may be located across several locations).

## 6. Expected Outputs
- All available requested items picked accurately according to the approved picking instruction.
- Each picked line checked and confirmed against the picking instruction.
- Inventory/location records updated and kept accurate following the physical pick.
- Completed pick transferred to the designated staging/packing area, ready for the next fulfilment stage.

## 7. Procedure

**Step 1: Receive Picking Assignment**
1.1 The Warehouse Supervisor or Manager assigns the picking task (the multi-item picking wave) to a designated operator or picking team.
1.2 The Storekeeper/Picker receives the approved picking instruction, which specifies product references, required quantities, and storage locations.

**Step 2: Review Picking Instruction**
2.1 The Storekeeper/Picker reviews the picking instruction in full before beginning physical picking activity.
2.2 The Storekeeper/Picker confirms understanding of all product references, quantities, and storage locations listed.

**Step 3: Travel to Storage Locations and Pick Items**
3.1 The Storekeeper/Picker proceeds to each identified storage location referenced in the picking instruction.
3.2 At each location, the Storekeeper/Picker picks only the product references and quantities stated in the picking instruction.
3.3 If the order contains items from several locations, the Storekeeper/Picker continues this process across all required locations until all lines have been addressed.

**Step 4: Check Each Picked Line**
4.1 For each product picked, the Storekeeper/Picker checks the picked item and quantity against the corresponding line in the picking instruction.
4.2 Only quantities and references matching the authorized picking instruction are confirmed as correctly picked.

**Step 5: Maintain Inventory/Location Record Accuracy**
5.1 Following the physical movement of each item from its storage location, the Storekeeper/Picker ensures that inventory and location records reflect the pick accurately.
5.2 This step must be completed for each picked line to preserve the integrity of stock and location data.

**Step 6: Transfer Completed Pick**
6.1 Once all lines in the picking instruction have been picked and checked, the Storekeeper/Picker transfers the completed pick to the designated staging/packing area.
6.2 This transfer marks the handover point to the next order-fulfilment stage.

**Step 7: Supervisory Oversight**
7.1 The Warehouse Supervisor or Manager may review the completed picking activity to confirm it was carried out in accordance with the authorized picking instruction.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling

The scenario identifies the following constraints and potential deviation points; the following handling principles apply:

- **Unauthorized Items or Quantities:** Only product references and quantities stated in the picking instruction may be picked. If the Storekeeper/Picker identifies a discrepancy (e.g., an item is unavailable, damaged, or present in insufficient quantity at the storage location), this must be reported to the Warehouse Supervisor or Manager for resolution before proceeding, since the scenario does not specify a defined substitution or override procedure.
- **Mismatched Picked Line:** If a picked item or quantity does not match the corresponding line in the picking instruction during the checking step, the discrepancy must be flagged and escalated to the Warehouse Supervisor or Manager rather than transferred forward, as the scenario does not authorize deviation from the approved instruction.
- **Partial Availability:** The expected outcome specifies that "all available requested items" are picked, indicating that if certain items are not available, the picking of available items may proceed; however, the scenario does not specify a documented procedure for handling unavailable items, and this should be clarified with the Warehouse Supervisor or Manager.
- **Inventory/Location Record Discrepancies:** If, after physical movement, the inventory/location record cannot be confirmed as accurate, this must be raised to the Warehouse Supervisor or Manager, as maintaining accurate records is a stated requirement of this process.
- **Escalation Point:** In all cases of deviation, discrepancy, or exception not explicitly resolvable at the Storekeeper/Picker level, the issue must be escalated to the Warehouse Supervisor or Manager for review and resolution. The scenario does not specify further escalation paths beyond this role.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The Order Picking process is considered complete when:
- All available product references and quantities specified in the approved picking instruction have been picked from their assigned storage locations.
- Each picked line has been checked and confirmed against the picking instruction.
- Inventory/location records have been updated to remain accurate following the physical movement of picked items.
- The completed pick has been transferred to the designated staging/packing area for the next fulfilment stage.
