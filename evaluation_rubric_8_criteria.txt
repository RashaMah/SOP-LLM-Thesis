FINAL SOP EVALUATION RUBRIC — VERSION 1.0

Purpose:
This rubric evaluates generated logistics and warehouse Standard Operating
Procedures (SOPs) against the source process scenario.

Each criterion is scored independently from 1 to 5.

Maximum primary score: 40 points.


============================================================
C1 — COMPLETENESS
============================================================

Evaluate whether all operationally relevant information explicitly contained
in the source scenario is represented in the SOP, including required
activities, information, roles, conditions, and expected outcomes.

Do not penalize the SOP for information that was not provided in the source
scenario.


============================================================
C2 — CORRECT SEQUENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether procedural activities follow the operational dependencies
and sequence specified or implied by the source scenario.

Penalize contradictory, illogical, or operationally incorrect ordering.


============================================================
C3 — ROLE CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether responsibilities are clearly and consistently assigned to
the roles provided in the source scenario.

Penalize ambiguous responsibility, incorrect role assignment, or unsupported
new roles when these materially affect the procedure.


============================================================
C4 — INPUT / OUTPUT CLARITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether required inputs and information, as well as expected
outputs and results, are clearly identifiable and consistent with the
source scenario.


============================================================
C5 — EXCEPTION HANDLING
============================================================

Evaluate whether exceptions, deviations, abnormal conditions, and decision
points contained in the source scenario are correctly identified and
handled.

Do not penalize an SOP merely because its source scenario contains few
exceptions.


============================================================
C6 — CONSTRAINT ADHERENCE
============================================================

Evaluate whether explicit constraints and decision conditions contained in
the source scenario are preserved and respected throughout the SOP.


============================================================
C7 — GROUNDEDNESS / NO HALLUCINATION
============================================================

Evaluate whether operational claims in the SOP remain grounded in the
source scenario.

Penalize invented systems, roles, requirements, thresholds, documents,
technologies, approvals, rules, or other material operational details that
are unsupported by the scenario.

Do not penalize harmless paraphrasing, organizational formatting, or wording
changes that preserve the original operational meaning.


============================================================
C8 — USABILITY
============================================================

Evaluate whether the SOP is clear, structured, internally consistent,
unambiguous, and actionable for the intended warehouse or logistics user,
while remaining faithful to the source scenario.


============================================================
COMMON SCORING SCALE
============================================================

5 — FULLY SATISFIES
The SOP fully satisfies the criterion. No substantive deficiency is
identifiable.

4 — GOOD
The SOP satisfies the criterion with only a minor omission, ambiguity, or
weakness that does not materially affect the procedure.

3 — ADEQUATE
The SOP partially satisfies the criterion. One meaningful deficiency or
several minor deficiencies are present, but the SOP remains substantially
correct and usable on this dimension.

2 — POOR
Major deficiencies are present. Important information, logic, or
requirements are missing, unclear, or incorrect and materially affect this
dimension.

1 — VERY POOR
The SOP fundamentally fails the criterion, substantially contradicts the
source scenario, or omits or incorrectly handles most essential elements
relevant to the criterion.


============================================================
SCORING RULES
============================================================

1. Each criterion must be scored independently.

2. Only integer scores from 1 to 5 are permitted.

3. The source process scenario is the factual reference for evaluation.

4. External operational knowledge must not be used to impose requirements
   that are absent from the scenario.

5. Missing information that was never supplied in the source scenario must
   not be treated as an SOP deficiency.

6. Plausible but unsupported operational additions must not be rewarded and
   may reduce the Groundedness / No Hallucination score.

7. Verbosity, document length, or sophisticated formatting must not by
   themselves increase a score.

8. Reasonable paraphrasing is acceptable when the operational meaning is
   preserved.

9. Exception Handling is evaluated relative to the exceptions and decision
   conditions contained in the particular source scenario.

10. The primary total score is calculated as:

    C1 + C2 + C3 + C4 + C5 + C6 + C7 + C8

    Minimum = 8
    Maximum = 40

11. Individual criterion scores must be retained in addition to the total
    score.

12. Any optional branch/decision-logic analysis is reported separately and
    is not included in the primary 40-point score.
