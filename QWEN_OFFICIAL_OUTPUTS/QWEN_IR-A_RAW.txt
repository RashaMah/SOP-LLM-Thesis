# Standard Operating Procedure: Inventory Replenishment and Stock Realignment

## 1. SOP Title
Inventory Replenishment and Stock Realignment

## 2. Purpose
To replenish the active picking location from warehouse reserve stock before it becomes insufficient for expected picking activity, ensuring that inventory and location records remain accurate after the movement.

## 3. Scope
This procedure applies to operations within a warehouse environment that utilizes separate active picking locations and reserve storage. It is triggered when picking stock has fallen to a low level while reserve stock is available for the same item.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Storekeeper/Warehouse Staff:** Responsible for conducting stock reviews, determining when picking location quantities are low, verifying reserve stock availability, executing the stock transfer, and ensuring inventory/location records remain accurate.
*   **Warehouse or Store Manager:** Involved in the process. (Note: Specific managerial duties or approval steps are not defined in the provided process scenario.)

## 5. Required Inputs / Information
*   Current status of stock in active picking locations.
*   Availability of additional quantity of the same item in reserve storage.
*   Identification of the designated picking location.
*   Current inventory and location records.
*   Applicable stock-rotation rules.

## 6. Expected Outputs
*   The picking location contains sufficient stock for continued operations.
*   Both physical and recorded quantities remain aligned.
*   Inventory/location records accurately reflect the movement.

## 7. Procedure
1.  **Conduct Stock Review:** Warehouse personnel shall review stock levels during the scheduled stock review period.
2.  **Identify Low Stock:** Determine if the quantity of an item in its picking location has become low relative to expected picking activity.
3.  **Verify Reserve Availability:** Confirm that additional quantity of the same item is available in reserve storage.
4.  **Select Stock:** Identify the correct product and stock batch to be moved.
5.  **Apply Rotation Rules:** Ensure that any applicable stock-rotation rule is respected during the selection and movement process.
6.  **Execute Transfer:** Transfer the required stock from reserve storage to the designated picking location.
7.  **Update Records:** Adjust inventory and location records immediately following the movement to ensure they match the physical stock.

## 8. Exception and Deviation Handling
*   **Inventory Discrepancy:** The movement must not create an unexplained difference between physical and recorded inventory. If a discrepancy is detected during or after the transfer, the process must be halted until the difference is explained and resolved.
*   **Incorrect Product or Batch:** If the correct product or stock batch cannot be verified, the transfer must not proceed.
*   **Rotation Rule Violation:** If the movement would violate an applicable stock-rotation rule, the transfer must not proceed.
*   **Insufficient Reserve Stock:** If additional quantity is not available in reserve storage, the replenishment process cannot be completed for this item at this time.

## 9. Process Completion Criteria
The process is considered complete when:
1.  The picking location contains sufficient stock for continued operations.
2.  Physical quantities match recorded quantities (no unexplained differences exist).
3.  All inventory and location records have been updated to reflect the transfer.
