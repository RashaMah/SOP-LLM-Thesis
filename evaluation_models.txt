Evaluation design:
Multi-judge, scenario-grounded, rubric-based evaluation.

EVALUATION PROCEDURE

- Every generated SOP is evaluated independently by all three judges.
- Generator/model identity is hidden from the judges.
- Each judge receives the same source scenario.
- Each judge receives the same candidate SOP.
- Each judge receives the same frozen 8-criterion rubric and judge instructions.
- Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5.
- All individual judge scores are retained.
- Judge reasoning/justification is retained for auditability.
- The evaluation script calculates totals; judges do not calculate totals.
- Scores from different judges are not shown to one another.
- Judges do not debate or revise their scores based on another judge.
- Inter-judge agreement will be analyzed separately.
- A subset will subsequently undergo independent human validation.

Primary dataset:

48 generated SOPs
× 3 independent judges
= 144 judge evaluations

8 criterion scores per evaluation
= 1,152 individual criterion ratings.
